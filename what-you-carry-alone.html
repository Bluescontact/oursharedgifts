The inward faces

These are for you. Write them at full size, the way you’d say them to yourself. They don’t go into what you copy or print unless you tick them below. Short answers and blanks are fine.

The outward face

This is what you’d actually say to someone: what can come in, and what can go out. It can be smaller than the need. It’s what Copy and Print carry, with anything you tick below.

Anything from the inward faces you want seen

Off unless you tick it. What you tick goes out word for word. Clearing a face clears its tick.

What can be copied

This is exactly what leaves when you copy or print. It changes as you write.

The questions

The face names stay. The questions under them are yours to change.
