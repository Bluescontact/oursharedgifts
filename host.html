Host

Host Kevin, and NESI the bus, for a week or two.

Kevin travels and works from a self-built bus running on 15kWh of off-grid capacity — it needs a place to park, not a place to plug in. Hosting means offering land or a driveway for 1–2 weeks. What's exchanged for that is laid out plainly below, not left to guesswork.

What hosting looks like

The bus is largely self-sufficient — it doesn't need shore power or a dedicated hookup to function. What it needs from a host is space, and whatever access makes a 1–2 week stay workable for both sides.

Space

Room to park a self-built bus — level, reasonably accessible ground. Rural, semi-rural, or a large enough driveway all work.

Duration

Typically 1–2 weeks per stay. Shorter or longer by arrangement, discussed before confirming.

Access

Water top-off and a place to dispose of waste are appreciated but not required — the off-grid capacity covers power on its own.

The transactional floor

What each side can expect from a hosting arrangement, stated plainly rather than negotiated fresh every time.

Suggested rate / exchange [a number or a range, or "work-trade only" if that's the floor]

Cancellation / flexibility [your actual policy — how much notice either side needs]

How it works

  • Reach out via Contact with your location, available dates, and a bit about the space.
  • A short call or message thread to confirm fit — see Book if a call is easier than email.
  • Dates and terms get confirmed in writing before Kevin arrives.
  • After the stay, hosts are invited to leave a review below.
