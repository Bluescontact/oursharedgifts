Recognition Letter

A short letter back to the person who sponsored this hour.

This isn't a report and it isn't a repayment. They gave this hour without knowing who'd use it or what would happen in it. Write back what actually happened, in your own words — that's the only ask. Nothing is scored, rated, or measured against what the hour "should" have produced.

A few honest sentences cover it. If it helps to have a shape:

  • What you brought into the hour
  • What actually happened, or shifted
  • One true thing you'd want them to know — not a thank-you performance, just what's real

Dear [donor name],

[your letter]

Send it through the contact page and it goes to Kevin directly. Nothing about it is kept, tracked, or filed — it lands once, and that's the whole transaction.
