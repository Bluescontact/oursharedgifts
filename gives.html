How this site gives

The terms

A gift completes when nothing remains claimable. Weight is permitted. Handles are not.

Everything here is given that way. Read what's useful, take what applies, change it, pass it on. You don't owe a reply, a subscription, or a read of anything else. Nothing about your visit is tracked.

Paid work is a separate track and is not a gift. It's money for a defined thing, at an amount you set, agreed in writing first, and it's kept apart from this so the two never blur.

This site, against its own four

A site that holds people should pass the same four. Here is where this one stands, stated plainly, including what isn't done.

Differentiation a person can be distinguishable

There is more than one way in. You can write with whatever context you need to give, or book an hour if talking is easier. Neither one needs you to perform the need well.

Connection capacity can find need

A message goes to Kevin directly, and you say what you need in your own words. There's no list of his categories to fit it into. Kevin answers in writing; in his words, A no closes it cleanly, with nothing owed, from either side. To state: how soon an answer comes.

Boundaries a limit can be stated and received

Limits are stated here ahead of time, as facts, not given as answers to a request. A no costs nothing in either direction. What he offers is on his page. What he doesn't, in his words: To-do lists of someone else's chores, on-call repairs, routine chores, or hard labor traded for a place to stay.

Architecture the load has a named holder, with a named fallback

Kevin holds this site and what's promised on it. To state: the fallback, when he can't.

How it's made

Much of what's here was drafted with Claude, an AI, working from Kevin's own published writing and words. It becomes his voice when he chooses to share it as his, and he says how it was made.
