A room for the winter, in pieces

NovDecJanFeb

A place to stay for three months, maybe four: three 1-month stays or six 2-week stays. You hold only your own stretch.

For hosts

It doesn't have to be one host. A week here, two weeks there, a month somewhere else: any of it works, as long as the next place is lined up before I leave the one I'm in. You hold only your own stretch: you're not arranging what comes next, and you're not responsible for my leaving.

A room
A spare bedroom, for a named length of time, with an end date we both write down and room to extend if we both want to. I don't expect meals or company. What I'd bring: projects from the menu, not chores.
How it can work
As a gift, as an exchange for menu work, as a pre-funded agreement (you set an amount for the stay up front, and the work we agree on draws it down), or as rent paid by me or a supporter.
The bus
Room to park a full-size bus while I'm in a room, with you or somewhere else. She runs on her own power and needs level ground, not a hookup. It carries a 15 kWh battery bank, a 10 kW inverter, and solar.
An address
You let me use your address and send photos of my mail for a year. Or, as a transaction: a mail-forwarding service, paid by you or a supporter.
Ending
Either of us can end a stay with 72 hours' written notice. No is always a fine answer.

My work, and what others have taken up

I was journeyman in the elevator trade, and I owned and renovated a multifamily rental property alongside my apprenticeship. I left as a whistleblower. Prior to elevators, I was a pest control tech, and I also have experience in building aquaponic systems. I organized and taught a dance community, running the lesson, the music, and the door.

  • I had six wonderful hosts over the summer, and another six reached out after I posted here to offer space for next season.
  • I helped hosts with everything from chicken coops and shade cloth to reclaiming land from the blackberry invasion.
  • A labor journalist has since published an article on my story.

Public, and yours to check: the documentation of my own case, Setting the Floor, and NESI.

How this site receives

In the order I'd ask for them, because I would much rather have relationships where I can be seen as both giver and receiver.

  1. Meet one of the needs above. Pick it on my page and send it from Your side, or write to me.
  2. Invite me, or hire me. What I can share is on my page. Book an hour. You name the amount, up front. If it helps to have a reference point, the suggested donation is $50 an hour. Wherever you land is whole.
  3. Subscribe. Sign up as a paid subscriber, and you'll be helping me to build a floor for myself. Substack.
  4. Send something directly. If something here was worth something to you, you can send what feels right: Venmo @Kevin-Mears-11. Gifts buy nothing and bind no one. Paid work is at an amount you name.
