Who this is for

If you fund, commission, or partner on systems work

OurSharedGifts is legible to people who need to know what they're funding before they fund it: what's actually running, what it costs to run, and what it would take to grow. If that's you —

  • Foundations and funders looking for working prototypes in gift-economy or civic-repair infrastructure, not white papers alone.
  • Studios or teams who want a design consult on recognition mechanics or non-scoring feedback systems.
  • Readers and researchers who want applied systems-design writing that's cited inside running code, not shelved theory.
  • Individuals who want to support the work directly and watch it keep running.
